The Process

From first login to full access in three steps

  1. 1

    Start a module

    Pick up where you left off or begin Module 1. The coach greets you with context and a clear first task.

  2. 2

    Follow the conversation

    Work through each step interactively. Ask questions mid-task — the coach pulls from indexed docs and videos to answer in context.

  3. 3

    Complete & unlock

    Mark steps done and unlock the next module. After all 10, you graduate to full Canvas access and policy-based chat.
